# RT Primer Sequences for miR-29 Detection v2

## Design Date
2026-10-01

## Universal RT Primer (for all three miR-29 targets)

### RT-Primer-Universal
```
5'-AGTGCGTACTCGAGGCATCGATCAGTACGGATCG-3'
```
- **Length:** 35 nt
- **Function:** Reverse transcription primer with 3' adapter sequence
- **Binding region:** 3' adapter region of RT-cDNA construct
- **Notes:** This is a universal primer that works for all three miR-29 family members

## miRNA-specific RT Primers (alternative design)

If you need higher specificity, use these miRNA-specific RT primers:

### RT-Primer-miR29a-specific
```
5'-AGTGCGTACTCGAGGCATCGATCAGTACGGATCGTAACCG-3'
```
- **Length:** 41 nt
- **3' specific anchor:** `TAACCG` (complementary to miR-29a 3' end: `CGGUUA`)
- **Tm:** ~64°C
- **Use case:** Selective RT for miR-29a only

### RT-Primer-miR29b-specific
```
5'-AGTGCGTACTCGAGGCATCGATCAGTACGGATCGAACACU-3'
```
- **Length:** 41 nt
- **3' specific anchor:** `AACACU` (complementary to miR-29b 3' end: `AGUGUU`)
- **Tm:** ~63°C
- **Use case:** Selective RT for miR-29b only

### RT-Primer-miR29c-specific
```
5'-AGTGCGTACTCGAGGCATCGATCAGTACGGATCGTAACCG-3'
```
- **Length:** 41 nt
- **3' specific anchor:** `TAACCG` (complementary to miR-29c 3' end: `CGGUUA`)
- **Tm:** ~64°C
- **Use case:** Selective RT for miR-29c only
- **Note:** Same as miR-29a because they share the same 3' end

## RT Reaction Protocol

### Reaction Mix (20 μL total)
- RNA sample: 1-10 μL (containing miRNA)
- RT primer: 500 nM (final concentration)
- dNTP mix: 0.5 mM each
- SuperScript IV RT: 200 U
- 5× RT buffer: 4 μL
- DTT: 5 mM
- RNase inhibitor: 20 U
- Nuclease-free water: to 20 μL

### Reaction Conditions
1. **Annealing:** 65°C for 5 min, then ice for 2 min
2. **Reverse Transcription:** 50°C for 30 min
3. **Inactivation:** 80°C for 10 min
4. **Storage:** -20°C or proceed to RPA

## Quality Control

### Expected RT Product
- **Template:** miRNA (22-23 nt)
- **RT product:** cDNA with 5' adapter, miRNA-derived region, and 3' adapter
- **Expected size:** ~110 nt

### Verification
Can be verified by:
1. qPCR with adapter-specific primers
2. Gel electrophoresis (run on 2% agarose or PAGE)
3. Direct sequencing

## Storage and Handling

### Stock Solution
- Resuspend in nuclease-free water to 100 μM
- Aliquot into 10 μL portions
- Store at -80°C (stable for >1 year)

### Working Solution
- Dilute to 10 μM in nuclease-free water
- Store at -20°C (stable for 6 months)
- Avoid freeze-thaw cycles (>5 times)

## Troubleshooting

### Low RT Efficiency
- Check primer Tm and adjust annealing temperature
- Increase RT primer concentration to 1 μM
- Extend RT time to 60 min
- Use fresh SuperScript IV enzyme

### Non-specific Products
- Use miRNA-specific RT primers instead of universal
- Increase annealing temperature by 2-3°C
- Add hot-start RT enzyme
- Reduce RT primer concentration to 200 nM

### No Product
- Verify RNA quality (not degraded)
- Check RT primer sequence (order confirmation)
- Ensure proper thermocycler calibration
- Try a positive control (synthetic miRNA standard)
